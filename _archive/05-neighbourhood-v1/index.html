How ordering works

1 — Pick your day

The bar at the top sets when you're collecting. Everything on the site updates to match it, so you never see something you can't actually have.

2 — Order by 11am

Before 11am and you can collect the same day. After that the clock starts tomorrow, because the morning's baking is already spoken for.

3 — Choose a time

Two-hour collection windows from 7am. Each one has a limited number of slots so there's never a queue out the door.

4 — Or we'll bike it

Local delivery within three miles, mornings only, $6 — free over $45. We do not ship, and we're not going to pretend otherwise.
