The atelier

Three people, one oven, no shortcuts

We make between twelve and eighteen cakes a day. It is what fits in the oven, and what three people can finish by hand before the shop opens.

A full Saturday simply stops appearing. The catalogue shows what the oven can hold, which is the same number it held in 2016.

Ines trained at Le Cordon Bleu and spent six years in restaurant pastry before deciding she would rather make one thing properly. Daniel came from bread. Mei came from botanical illustration, which explains the roses.

Before you order

Ordering & lead times

How much notice do you need?

It depends on the cake, which is why we show it on every product rather than printing one rule at the bottom of the page. Midnight needs a day. Noir, Framboise and Caffè need two. Prism and Grand Chocolat need three. Jardin needs five because every flower is piped by hand. Winterberry needs a week.

Choosing hand-piped florals as a finish adds two days to whatever cake you put them on. The calendar updates the moment you select it.

What is the 2pm cutoff?

Orders placed before 2pm count today as the first day of the lead time. After 2pm the clock starts tomorrow, because the day's baking is already committed. The countdown in the bar at the top of every page is showing you exactly how long is left.

Why are some dates crossed out?

Those days are fully booked. We cap the number of cakes we take so that the last one out of the kitchen is as good as the first. Weekends fill first, usually two to three weeks ahead in spring and December.

Delivery or pickup?

Both. Pickup is from 114 Wythe Avenue in four two-hour windows between 9am and 5pm. Delivery is by our own couriers, chilled, between 9am and 2pm — $12 across Brooklyn and Lower Manhattan, $18 for greater Manhattan and Queens, and free over $150.

Can you write on the cake?

On every cake except Winterberry, whose surface is given over to fruit. You will see exactly what we will pipe, in the lettering we will use, before you add it to your order. Forty characters, which is more than it sounds.

Allergies

Present in the kitchen at all times: wheat, dairy, egg, nuts, soy. No plate in this catalogue is certified free of any of them, and none can be. Requirements noted at checkout are confirmed by Ines before the cake is scheduled, not after.
