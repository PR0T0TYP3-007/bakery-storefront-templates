How ordering works

  1. 1 — Pick your day

    The bar at the top sets when you're collecting. Everything on the site updates to match it, so you never see something you can't actually have.

    2 — Order by 11am

    Before 11 and you can collect the same day. After that the clock starts tomorrow, because the morning's baking is already spoken for.

  2. 3 — Choose a time

    Two-hour collection windows from half seven. Each one holds a limited number of bags, so there's never a queue out of the door.

    4 — Or we'll bike it

    Local delivery within three miles, mornings only. We don't ship, and we're not going to pretend otherwise.
