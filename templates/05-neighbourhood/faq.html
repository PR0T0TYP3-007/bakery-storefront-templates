Questions we get asked

How much notice do you need?

It depends on the item, so every product page tells you its own. Bread and pastry are same-day if you order by 11am. Celebration cakes need two days, three if there is fresh fruit on top.

Some options add time of their own. Hand-modelled fondant figures add four days to whatever cake they go on, and the calendar updates the moment you pick them.

What’s the 11am cutoff?

Order by 11am and today counts as day one. After that we start you from tomorrow. The bar at the top of every page counts down to it, so you’re never guessing.

Why can’t I pick some days?

Those ones are full. We only take so many a day so the last order out is as good as the first. Weekends go first — usually two or three weeks ahead in spring and December, so it’s worth booking early.

Why does the board show counts on some days and not others?

Because we only honestly know one of them. For today we can tell you what’s actually on the shelf, and the number falls through the day as things sell. For any day still to come there is no shelf yet, so all we can give you is the size of that morning’s batch.

We’d rather tell you that than make a number up.

Allergens

—

Every product page lists what that item contains, including anything introduced by an option you choose — the carrot and walnut sponge brings nuts with it, for example. If you tell us about an allergy in the notes at checkout and we can’t safely make what you’ve asked for, we will call you rather than guess.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Give us a ring. Up to 48 hours before collection we can usually change or refund a celebration cake. Inside 48 hours the ingredients are bought and your slot’s held, so we can’t refund then — but we can nearly always move it to another day.
