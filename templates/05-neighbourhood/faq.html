Ordering & allergens

How much notice do you need?

It depends on the item, which is why every product says so on its own page rather than us printing one rule at the bottom of the site. Bread and pastry are same-day if you order before 11am. Celebration cakes need two days, three if there is fresh fruit on top.

Some options add time of their own. Hand-modelled fondant figures add four days to whatever cake they go on, and the calendar updates the moment you pick them.

What does the cutoff mean?

Order before 11am and today counts as the first day of the lead time. After that the clock starts tomorrow, because the morning's baking is already committed. The bar at the top of every page counts down to it.

Why are some days crossed out?

Those days are full. We cap how many orders we take so the last one out of the kitchen is as good as the first. Weekends fill first — usually two to three weeks ahead in spring and December.

Why does the board show counts on some days and not others?

Because we only honestly know one of them. For today we can tell you what is actually on the shelf, and the number falls through the day as things sell. For any day still to come there is no shelf yet, so all we can give you is the size of that morning's batch.

We would rather say that than invent a number.

Allergens

—

Every product page lists what that item contains, including anything introduced by an option you choose — the carrot and walnut sponge brings nuts with it, for example. If you tell us about an allergy in the notes at checkout and we cannot safely make what you have asked for, we will call you rather than guess.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Call the shop. Up to 48 hours before collection we can usually change or refund a celebration cake. Inside 48 hours the ingredients are bought and the slot is held, so we cannot refund — but we can nearly always move it to a different day.
