Style guide

Every component against deliberately awkward content, so the states that only appear on the wrong day — or with the wrong text in them — are all visible in one place rather than discovered in production.

Colour

The page runs on sun, ink and grape. The confetti colours are confined to the cake, to illustration and to status — never to decoration.

Contrast

Measured live from the tokens, not copied from a spreadsheet. Anything below 4.5:1 for body text is a bug.
